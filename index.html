Podcast Bot

Full podcast episodes in, short clips out.

Podcast Bot is a personal tool I run on my own computer. It turns my podcast episodes into short vertical clips with subtitles, and I decide which ones get posted.

  • I add a full episodeThe tool transcribes it and finds the strongest moments.
  • It prepares the clipsVertical format, subtitles, a title and a thumbnail for each one.
  • I review every clipNothing is posted until I approve it.
  • Approved clips go to my accountsOn TikTok they land in my drafts, and I publish them myself in the TikTok app.
  • I see what worksView and like counts of my own videos help me make better clips next time.

Who can use it

Only me. Podcast Bot is not a public service and does not access anyone else's account.
