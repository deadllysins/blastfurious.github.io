Podcast Bot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Summary

Podcast Bot is a personal tool used only by its owner. It does not collect data from other people, does not sell data and does not share data with anyone.

Data from TikTok

When the owner connects their own TikTok account, Podcast Bot receives:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic), to confirm which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos (video.upload), to send approved clips to the owner's TikTok drafts.
  • A list of the owner's own videos with their view, like, comment and share counts (video.list), to show which clips perform best.

Where data is stored

All data, including login tokens and video statistics, is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not uploaded to any other server.

How long data is kept

Data is kept while the tool is in use. The owner can delete it at any time by removing the tool's data folder, and can disconnect TikTok at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy.

Other people's data

Podcast Bot does not access, collect or store information about other TikTok users.
